Five steps to a defensible case

  1. Observe: state what is visible or recorded.
  2. Record: retain source, event time, timezone and recorder.
  3. Verify: compare IDs, quantities, amounts and approvals.
  4. Escalate: record the recipient, uncertainty and next action.
  5. Close: record the finding, outcome, reviewer and time.
