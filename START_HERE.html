What do the unfamiliar words mean?
QAQuality assurance: checking work and recording what you find.
VerificationChecking an observation against relevant records.
EscalationAsking an authorized supervisor or colleague to take action.
DispositionThe written outcome explaining how a case was closed.
Carry-overAn older case that still needs attention.
UTCA common time reference for comparing records from different timezones.
JSON backupA complete text copy of the records and history. There is no automatic restore button.
